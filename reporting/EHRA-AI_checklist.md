# Supplementary Table S2. EHRA Artificial Intelligence Reporting Checklist

*MultiQRSNet: Explainable Deep Learning for ECG-Based Classification of Outflow Tract Ventricular Premature Contraction Origin. Completed per the EHRA 2025 State-of-the-Art Document on Artificial Intelligence in Clinical Electrophysiology (Svennberg E, et al. Europace 2025;27(5):euaf071). "Location" refers to manuscript sections.*

| **Item**                 | **Element**                                    | **How addressed in this study**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | **Location**                                |
|-------|----------------------|----------------------------------------|-------------|
| | **TITLE & INTRODUCTION** | | |
| i                        | **Title**                                      | Identified as an explainable deep-learning (1-D convolutional neural network) study for ECG-based classification of outflow-tract VPC origin.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Title                                       |
| 1                        | **Intended clinical use**                      | Pre-procedural, non-invasive classification of outflow-tract VPC origin (RVOT vs LVOT; exploratory intramural) from the 12-lead surface ECG to support ablation planning.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Abstract; Introduction                      |
| 2                        | **Clinical benefit**                           | Explored against standard care: in an exploratory head-to-head comparison (Supplementary Table S3), two blinded electrophysiologists manually applying three established ECG algorithms (V2 transition ratio, V2S/V3R index, transition-zone index) reached 67.0% and 68.0% accuracy versus the model's out-of-fold 95.1% (paired McNemar p\<10⁻⁸).                                                               | Supplementary Table S3                           |
| | **METHODS** | | |
| 3                        | **Data collection**                            | Retrospective. Development: single centre. Independent external validation: separate clinical cohort. Additional external validation: public Zheng et al. database.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Methods 2.1, 2.3                            |
| 4                        | **Source of data**                             | Consecutive patients undergoing catheter ablation for symptomatic/frequent VPCs; standard 12-lead ECG and CARTO-3 digital recordings.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Methods 2.1, 2.3                            |
| 5                        | **Development (training) data set**            | 129 patients (RVOT 58, LVOT 45, intramural 26); binary task 103. Each recording 2500 samples/lead at 1 kHz (2.5 s), aligned on the index VPC.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Methods 2.1, 2.3                            |
| 6                        | **Participants**                               | Inclusion: symptomatic/frequent VPC ablation, Jul 2020--Jul 2025. Exclusion: 30 non-outflow-tract ablations; structural heart disease excluded. Flow in Figure 1.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Methods 2.1; Figure 1                       |
| 7                        | **Comparator (ground truth)**                  | Reference standard = origin confirmed by electroanatomic mapping and ablation, set by blinded re-adjudication of all cases independent of the model. Intramural defined by procedural surrogate criteria (insufficient bilateral EAM and/or ablation at two adjacent regions).                                                                                                                               | Methods 2.2                                 |
| 8                        | **Testing data set**                           | Patient-level, stratified group five-fold cross-validation (StratifiedGroupKFold, seed 42); no patient shared across folds. Internal estimate from out-of-fold predictions (103 patients). External cohorts --- independent clinical (46) and open-source (334) --- were each evaluated once with the fully locked model and were NOT used for model development, tuning, threshold or checkpoint selection. | Methods 2.4                                 |
| 9                        | **Sample size**                                | Determined by available consecutive cases; class counts reported; no formal power calculation. Small sample (especially intramural, n=26) acknowledged as a limitation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Methods; Discussion 4.1                        |
| 10                       | **Outcome**                                    | Patient-level classification: binary RVOT-vs-LVOT and exploratory three-class, reported with accuracy, AUROC, AUPRC, balanced accuracy, macro-F1 and 95% CIs (not single p-values).                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Results; Table 2                            |
| 11                       | **Data type**                                  | Digital 12-lead ECG signals (not images). Final model input is a 24-channel tensor (12 robustly normalized leads + 12 first-order derivatives) plus a 12-dimensional MDI vector.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Methods 2.3                                 |
| 12                       | **Data preparation**                           | A single, pre-specified preprocessing pipeline (median centring and interquartile-range scaling of each lead and derivative channels) applied identically to every dataset; fixed before external data were analysed. Patient-level split prevents leakage.                                                                                                                                                   | Methods 2.3                                 |
| 13                       | **Balanced groups**                            | Class distribution reported; label-smoothing cross-entropy, signal-only Mixup and augmentation to mitigate imbalance; balanced accuracy and macro-F1 reported for the imbalanced three-class task.                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Methods 2.4; Results                        |
| 14                       | **Data issues (noise/missing/outliers)**       | ECG-specific preprocessing addresses baseline drift and low-frequency interference; robust normalization limits outlier influence.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Methods 2.3                                 |
| 15                       | **Feature engineering**                        | Automatic feature learning by the 1-D CNN plus first-order derivatives. A 12-dimensional Maximal Deflection Index (MDI) vector is fused with the learned features in the reported model; a post hoc input ablation showed no consistent incremental benefit (Supplementary Table S4).                                                                                                                             | Methods; Supplementary Table S4                  |

| | **RESULTS** | | |
| 22                       | **Participants (results)**                     | Internal baseline demographics in Table 1. Independent external clinical cohort composition: RVOT 23, LVOT 23 (n=46 binary; 4 intramural excluded); open-source 334 (RVOT 257, LVOT 77).                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Results 3.1; Table 1; Figure 1              |
| 23                       | **Training performance**                       | Fixed 50-epoch training without checkpoint selection; run-to-run variability of the internal estimate reported (90.3–95.1%); no claim of absent overfitting. A gradient-boosted (XGBoost) baseline is reported for context.                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Results                                     |
| 24                       | **Internal validation**                        | Patient-level five-fold cross-validation: binary accuracy 95.1% (95% CI 90.3--99.0), AUROC 0.978 (independent repeated run of the same configuration 90.3%); three-class balanced accuracy 62.7%, macro-F1 62.1%, intramural recall 23.1%.                                                                                                                                                                   | Results; Table 2; Supplementary Table S4         |
| 25                       | **External validation**                        | Independent clinical cohort (n=46) accuracy 87.0% (95% CI 76.1--95.7); open-source cohort (n=334) accuracy 82.6% (95% CI 78.4--86.8).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Results; Table 2                            |
| 26                       | **Model performance (metrics)**                | Patient-level accuracy, AUROC, AUPRC, sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, balanced accuracy, macro-F1 and Brier score reported with confusion structure (Table 2; Supplementary Table S4). Reader comparison in Supplementary Table S3.                                                                                                                                                                                | Table 2; Table 3; Supplementary Tables S3 and S4            |
| | **DISCUSSION** | | |
| 27                       | **Performance errors**                         | Model errors (out-of-fold): 3 LVOT and 2 RVOT patients. Reader errors concentrated in LVOT origins with RVOT-like morphology (pseudo-RVOT cusp cases).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Results; Supplementary Table S3                  |
| 28                       | **Performance vs classic statistical methods** | Compared with a gradient-boosted (XGBoost) baseline (descriptive; study not powered for superiority) and, in Supplementary Table S3, with two blinded electrophysiologists applying three established ECG algorithms (paired McNemar).                                                                                                                                                                            | Results; Supplementary Table S3                  |
| 29                       | **Generalizability**                           | Transportability discussed across independent clinical and open-source datasets; limits noted (single-centre development, small sample, retrospective design); prospective multicentre validation stated as required.                                                                                                                                                                                        | Discussion; Discussion 4.1                     |
