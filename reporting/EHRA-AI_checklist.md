# Supplementary Table S2. EHRA Artificial Intelligence Reporting Checklist

*MultiQRSNet: Explainable Deep Learning for ECG-Based Classification of Outflow Tract Premature Ventricular Complex Origin. Completed per the EHRA 2025 State-of-the-Art Document on Artificial Intelligence in Clinical Electrophysiology (Svennberg E, et al. Europace 2025;27(5):euaf071). “Location” refers to manuscript sections.*

| **Item** | **Element**                                    | **How addressed in this study**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | **Location**                                     |
|----------|------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
|          | **TITLE & INTRODUCTION**                       |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |                                                  |
| i        | **Title**                                      | Identified as an explainable deep-learning (1-D convolutional neural network) study for ECG-based classification of outflow-tract PVC origin.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Title                                            |
| 1        | **Intended clinical use**                      | Pre-procedural, non-invasive classification of outflow-tract PVC origin (RVOT vs LVOT; exploratory intramural) from the 12-lead surface ECG to support ablation planning.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Abstract; Introduction                           |
| 2        | **Clinical benefit**                           | Explored against standard care: in an exploratory head-to-head comparison (Supplementary Table S3), two blinded electrophysiologists manually applying three established ECG algorithms (V2 transition ratio, V2S/V3R index, transition-zone index) reached 67.0% and 68.0% accuracy versus the model’s out-of-fold 91.3% (paired McNemar p \< 0.001).                                                   | Supplementary Table S3                           |
|          | **METHODS**                                    |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |                                                  |
| 3        | **Data collection**                            | Retrospective. Development: single centre. Independent external validation: separate clinical cohort. Additional external validation: public Zheng et al. database.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Methods 2.1, 2.3                                 |
| 4        | **Source of data**                             | Consecutive patients undergoing catheter ablation for symptomatic/frequent PVCs; standard 12-lead ECG and CARTO-3 digital recordings.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Methods 2.1, 2.3                                 |
| 5        | **Development (training) data set**            | 129 patients (RVOT 58, LVOT 45, intramural 26); binary task 103. Each recording 2500 samples/lead at 1 kHz (2.5 s), aligned on the index PVC.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Methods 2.1, 2.3                                 |
| 6        | **Participants**                               | Inclusion: symptomatic/frequent PVC ablation, Jul 2020–Jul 2025. Exclusion: 30 non-outflow-tract ablations; structural heart disease excluded. Flow in Figure 1.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Methods 2.1; Figure 1                            |
| 7        | **Comparator (ground truth)**                  | Reference standard = origin confirmed by electroanatomic mapping and ablation, set by blinded re-adjudication of all cases independent of the model. Intramural defined by procedural surrogate criteria (insufficient bilateral EAM and/or ablation at two adjacent regions).                                                                                                                           | Methods 2.2                                      |
| 8        | **Testing data set**                           | Patient-level, stratified group five-fold cross-validation (StratifiedGroupKFold, seed 42); no patient shared across folds. Internal estimate from out-of-fold predictions (103 patients). External cohorts — independent clinical (46) and open-source (334) — were each evaluated once with the fully locked model and were NOT used for model development, tuning, threshold or checkpoint selection. | Methods 2.4                                      |
| 9        | **Sample size**                                | Determined by available consecutive cases; class counts reported; no formal power calculation. Small sample (especially intramural, n=26) acknowledged as a limitation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Methods; Discussion 4.1                          |
| 10       | **Outcome**                                    | Patient-level classification: binary RVOT-vs-LVOT and exploratory three-class, reported with accuracy, AUROC, AUPRC, balanced accuracy, macro-F1 and 95% CIs (not single p-values).                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Results; Table 2                                 |
| 11       | **Data type**                                  | Digital 12-lead ECG signals (not images). Final model input is a 24-channel tensor (12 robustly normalized leads + 12 first-order derivatives).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Methods 2.3                                      |
| 12       | **Data preparation**                           | A single, pre-specified preprocessing pipeline (median centring and interquartile-range scaling of each lead and derivative channels) applied identically to every dataset; fixed before external data were analysed. Patient-level split prevents leakage.                                                                                                                                              | Methods 2.3                                      |
| 13       | **Balanced groups**                            | Class distribution reported; label-smoothing cross-entropy, signal-only Mixup and augmentation to mitigate imbalance; balanced accuracy and macro-F1 reported for the imbalanced three-class task.                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Methods 2.4; Results                             |
| 14       | **Data issues (noise/missing/outliers)**       | ECG-specific preprocessing addresses baseline drift and low-frequency interference; robust normalization limits outlier influence.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Methods 2.3                                      |
| 15       | **Feature engineering**                        | Automatic feature learning by the 1-D CNN plus first-order derivatives. A 12-dimensional timing feature used in the original submission was removed after a post hoc analysis showed no incremental value (Supplementary Table S4).                                                                                                                                                                      | Methods; Supplementary Table S4                  |

|          | **RESULTS**                                    |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |                                                  |
| 22       | **Participants (results)**                     | Internal baseline demographics in Table 1. | 23       | **Training performance**                       | Fixed 50-epoch training without checkpoint selection; run-to-run variability reported over five seeds per configuration (final model 87.4–92.2%; Supplementary Table S6); no claim of absent overfitting. | 25       | **External validation**                        | Independent clinical cohort (n=46) accuracy 87.0% (95% CI 76.1–95.7); open-source cohort (n=334) accuracy 81.4% (95% CI 77.2–85.6); across five seeds, 85.7 ± 1.9% and 83.6 ± 1.5%, respectively.                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Results; Table 2                                 |
